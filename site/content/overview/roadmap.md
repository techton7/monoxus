title: Roadmap
description: Status of all 44 Monoxus headless primitives across implemented, Batch 2, and Batch 3 waves.
---

Monoxus tracks 44 headless primitives across its baseline release and Track 2 expansion waves. **30 primitives are currently implemented**, with 6 planned for Batch 2 and 8 planned for Batch 3.

## Summary

| Wave | Count | Status | Scope |
|---|---:|---|---|
| **Implemented (Baseline + Track 2 Batches 1A–5A)** | 30 | Implemented | Core controls, overlays, forms, display, slider, scroll area, hover card, toolbar, pagination, pin input, a11y utilities |
| **Batch 2 (Tier 3B–3C & Tier 4B)** | 6 | Planned | Menus (`dropdown_menu`, `context_menu`, `menubar`), `navigation_menu`, `command`, `combobox` |
| **Batch 3 (Tier 5B–5C)** | 8 | Planned | `calendar`, `range_calendar`, segmented date/time fields, `date_picker`, `date_range_picker` |
| **Total** | **44** | | |
